5. STAGE-MACHINE IDENTIFICATION ($ce) — HIGH for the function, MEDIUM for
   the exact per-stage tick math (20 956-byte state machine; full decode
   not completed this pass)
====================================================================
$ce(Battle, Unit) @0x47501f0..0x47553cc (siege-ce-fe-windows.txt):
  - calls $hi(Battle,Unit,int,int) @0x4831fe4 — the siege helper family
    already resolved in v=30 (ACT_SIEGE_TO/FROM arm helpers $hi/$Yh)
  - calls $be(Battle,Unit) @0x474ce70 between transform steps
  - repeatedly loads a short from the unit's UnitType-shaped object and
    compares it against scaled tick products (mul w8,w8,w9 / cmp) — the
    tick_to_spec/tick_from_spec consumption shape
  - writes siegeTick (0xA4) and reads walk/siege interplay fields
$fe(Battle, Unit) @0x475afa4..0x475c2d4: second siege-relevant consumer
  (auto-siege / after-walk timer shape: siegeAfterWalkTick writes).
Documented verdict: durations = UnitType.tick_to_spec (to-siege) /
  tick_from_spec (from-siege) in sim ticks, progression through the three
  named stages, siege_blocked gating during the transform, stage+tick
  server-authoritative and streamed. The precise stage boundary split
  (how tick_to_spec divides across stages 0/1/2) remains MEDIUM.
